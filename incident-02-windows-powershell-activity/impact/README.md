# Impact Assessment – PowerShell Activity

## Impact Objective
Assess the potential impact of the suspicious PowerShell activity on the
affected system and the broader environment.

## Affected Assets
- Endpoint: Single Windows workstation
- User account: Standard user
- Network scope: No evidence of lateral movement

## Impact Analysis
At this stage of the investigation:
- No confirmed system compromise has been identified
- No evidence of data exfiltration observed
- No persistence mechanisms detected

However, the use of PowerShell introduces potential risks, including:
- Execution of malicious scripts
- Unauthorized system changes
- Preparation for follow-on actions

## Business Impact
- No operational disruption observed
- No business services affected
- Potential risk remains limited to the affected endpoint

## Risk Assessment
- Impact level: Low to Medium
- Rationale:
  - Suspicious activity observed
  - Limited scope and duration
  - No confirmed malicious outcome

## Impact Conclusion
The current impact is assessed as limited. Continued monitoring and
appropriate response actions are required to prevent potential escalation.
